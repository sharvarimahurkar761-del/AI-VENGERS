# FAQ: How to Export Your Data

We believe your data belongs to you. PulseIQ provides comprehensive data export capabilities, allowing you to easily download your analytics, customer logs, and system metrics at any time.

### Exporting from the Dashboard
To export data from a specific dashboard view:
1. Navigate to the desired dashboard (e.g., "User Analytics").
2. Adjust the date range filters in the top right corner to reflect the timeframe you want to export.
3. Click the **"Export"** button located next to the date picker.
4. Select your preferred format: **CSV**, **JSON**, or **PDF report**.
5. Your download will begin automatically. For larger datasets spanning multiple months, the system may process the request in the background and send you an email with a download link once it is ready.

### Bulk Export (Entire Account)
If you are an Administrator and need a complete dump of all your account data for compliance or migration purposes:
1. Go to **Settings > Workspace > Data Management**.
2. Under the "Data Export" section, click **"Request Full Export"**.
3. You will be prompted to verify your identity using two-factor authentication (2FA).
4. The system will compile a compressed `.zip` archive containing all JSON logs and CSV tables. Depending on the size of your workspace, this process can take anywhere from 10 minutes to a few hours. 
5. You will receive an email notification containing a secure, time-sensitive download link valid for 48 hours.
