# FAQ: How to Reset Your Password

If you have forgotten your password or simply need to change it for security reasons, resetting it in PulseIQ is a simple and straightforward process. Please follow these steps to securely reset your password and regain access to your account:

1. Navigate to the PulseIQ login page at `https://app.pulseiq.com/login`.
2. Below the login form, click on the **"Forgot Password?"** link. This will redirect you to the password reset request page.
3. Enter the email address associated with your PulseIQ account and click the **"Send Reset Link"** button.
4. Check your email inbox. You should receive an email from PulseIQ Support with a secure password reset link within a few minutes. (If you don't see it, be sure to check your Spam or Junk folder).
5. Click the link in the email. You will be taken to a secure page where you can enter and confirm your new password.
6. Make sure your new password meets our security requirements: at least 12 characters, including one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
7. Click **"Update Password"**. You will be redirected to the login screen where you can now log in with your new credentials.

If you no longer have access to the email address associated with your account, please contact our support team at support@pulseiq.com for further assistance. We will require additional verification to ensure the security of your account before making any changes.
